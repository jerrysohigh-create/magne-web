What These Signals Mean

For Users

MAGNE is not a grey-market or unbranded device. It has internationally recognized identifiers, certified safety testing, and platform compatibility that can be independently verified. MAGNE is designed for broad international compatibility across supported LTE and 5G bands, while actual carrier availability may vary by region, operator, SKU, software configuration, and certification status.

For Partners & Channels

Distribution partners, carriers, and retail channels can verify MAGNE's regulatory standing through public databases. The certification portfolio reduces channel risk and supports faster regulatory clearance for inventory and sales authorization.

For Market Readiness

The combination of GMS, FCC, CB, UN38.3, and CE — now certified (EU-Type Examination Cert No. SN26C0174) — confirms that MAGNE is materially closer to shippable status in major markets. MAGNE is presented as a certification-active, production-oriented device program rather than a concept-only prototype.
