What problem does this information solve?

CoverRF / EMC / SAR / HAC

The FCC Documentation Center is used to verify the radio frequency and electromagnetic compatibility public submissions of MAG1 related to the U.S. market.

SAR and HAC type documents are commonly used for health safety, accessibility and regulatory review; they should be read in conjunction with specific test reports and public repository results.

The FCC ID is the core verification index for this set of information and can be used by partners to cross-check against official FCC systems.
