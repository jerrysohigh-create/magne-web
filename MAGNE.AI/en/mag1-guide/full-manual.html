USB-C / compatible wireless charger → Settings → Battery

Before you start

  • Compatible, undamaged charger, cable or wireless charging pad
  • Dry device and ports
  • Remove metal objects and incompatible cases before wireless charging

Steps

  1. Wired: connect an undamaged cable to USB-C and a compatible charger, then check for the on-screen charging indicator.
  2. Wireless: align the center of the phone’s back as directed by the charging-pad instructions, keep it in contact and check for charging status. The alignment position has not been tested.

  4. Stop charging if there is overheating, an unusual smell, swelling, liquid ingress or port damage, and contact a qualified repair service.
  5. When finished, unplug by holding the connector rather than pulling the cable. Lift the phone off the pad to end wireless charging.
