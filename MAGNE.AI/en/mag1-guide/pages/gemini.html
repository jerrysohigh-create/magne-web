MAGNE, AI and Web3 / Gemini

Gemini: side key, live screen sharing and webpage summaries

Open Gemini over a public webpage, share the screen in Live, or request a readable webpage summary.

MAG1WW60-1 · dated captures available

This chapter uses MAG1WW60-1 evidence only. Reference steps are separate from the actions actually recorded.

Press & hold power button → Gemini → Live / Summarize page

MAG1WW60-1 actual result

The current test reached Gemini Live and Android screen-sharing consent. The active projection and its termination were checked. The ended Live transcript displayed “To use Gemini Live, update the Google app to the latest version.” This text alone does not establish the cause or prove that an update is required. A successful Live answer or webpage summary was not obtained; do not treat this recording as a completed assistant task.

Before you start

  • Google/Gemini assistant availability depends on the installed app, account, region and network. This capture used the assistant supplied by the Google app.
  • Use a public webpage and check that no messages, account details or sensitive notifications are visible before sharing. The recording shows a public MAGNE.AI page.
  • Check Settings → System → Gestures → Press & hold power button → Digital assistant. This setting was already selected and was left unchanged.

Recorded sequence

  1. Display a public MAGNE.AI webpage in Chrome.
  2. Invoke the assistant, open Gemini Live, select screen sharing, review Entire screen and tap Start. The recording uses ADB assistant-key input; physical power-button timing is not tested.
  3. Scroll the public page, return to Live and end the conversation. Check that screen sharing has stopped.

Observed controls

  • Digital assistant: the selected long-press power-button assignment.
  • Open Gemini Live and screen-sharing control: open the Live interface and Android sharing consent. Entire screen and Start appear in the consent dialog.
  • Close the conversation: end Live. Verify sharing has stopped before opening personal content.

MAG1WW60-1 observed scope

Android Entire screen sharing consent on MAG1WW60-1; the demonstration shares a public MAGNE.AI webpage. The ended Live transcript displays "To use Gemini Live, update the Google app to the latest version." This transcript alone does not diagnose an app-version or firmware fault; a useful webpage answer was not demonstrated. MAG1WW60-1: ADB assistant-key invocation and public-page screen sharing; active and stopped projection verified. This silent recording demonstrates sharing controls, not a successful webpage answer. Physical side-button timing was not tested. Power-button assignment and hold-duration options inspected; setting unchanged; physical press timing not tested

Restoration

Screen sharing ended. The temporary microphone-capture restriction was restored to its original setting. The standalone Gemini package remains in its initial disabled state; the recorded assistant was supplied through the Google app.

Troubleshooting

  • If the power menu appears, check the long-press assignment and default assistant. The ADB recording does not test a physical button press.
  • This session displayed a request to update the Google app. Check its version and service availability before repeating the test; this message alone does not establish a firmware defect.
  • Microphone capture was temporarily restricted for this silent demonstration and then restored. Voice interaction was not tested.

Test boundary

Applies to the Google/Gemini state observed on this MAG1WW60-1 device. Service availability also depends on the app version, account and network. Speech quality, offline inference, connected personal services and a successful summary are not verified.
