UN38.3 testing is an important basis for lithium battery transportation safety and is used to support document review in logistics scenarios such as air transportation, sea transportation, and land transportation.

MSDS, UN Test, Summary, package drop and stacking documents jointly describe the safety background of the battery and packaging under shipping conditions.

UN38.3 does not replace CB, FCC, CE, GMS and other documents; it is a layer of evidence for the safety of logistics and battery transportation.
