I

CERTIFICATE ROLE

What problem does this information solve?

TAC01681300
identifyDevice identity
useIMEI/Model Identity Reference
data hierarchyGSMA TAC certificate

TAC is the Type Allocation Code of the first 8 digits of IMEI, which is used to identify the mobile device model and device identity.

For carriers, channels, compliance and supply chain reviews, TAC can help link device identity with product data, certification documents and market records.

The TAC reference for MAG1 should be read in conjunction with market access documents such as model number, product configuration, FCC/CE, etc.
