CERTIFICATE ROLE

What problem does this information solve?

marketCalifornia
themeChemical exposure disclosure
documentTUV Rheinland CP65 Report
useConsumer Disclosure and Channel Review

California Proposition 65 requires companies to provide warnings or demonstrate compliance background for exposure to certain chemicals.

MAG1’s CP65 report can be used to support channel, platform and consumer compliance reviews.

It is different from wireless, battery, and security chip documents, focusing on chemical substance disclosure and consumer product requirements.
