System settings / sound

Media, call, ringtone and notification volume; vibration

Treat each volume source separately. Raising media volume does not necessarily make incoming calls audible.

MAG1WW60-1 · dated captures available

This chapter uses MAG1WW60-1 evidence only. Reference steps are separate from the actions actually recorded.

Settings → Sound & vibration

Before you start

  • Device unlocked
  • Avoid testing sounds where quiet is required
  • Start headphones at a low volume

Reference steps · see recorded scope below

View dated screenshots and videos ↓

  1. Open Sound & vibration and inspect the volume sliders shown on the device.
  2. Adjust the slider for the relevant source: Media volume for playback, Ring volume for incoming calls, or Alarm volume for alarms. Confirm the exact labels against the screenshot.
  3. To change a ringtone, open Phone ringtone, preview it, then confirm. Use a non-sensitive test file for custom audio.
  4. Open Vibration & haptics to view call, notification and touch-feedback options. Do not assume support for options that are not shown.
  5. Return to settings and check whether Do Not Disturb is suppressing sound or output is going to headphones or a Bluetooth device.

Cancel, exit and restore

Restore the original volume, ringtone and vibration settings. Stop any preview and go back.

Troubleshooting

  • Only one app is silent: check its own settings and notification channels
  • Speaker is silent: disconnect headphones or Bluetooth and check the output again
  • Incoming calls are silent: check Do Not Disturb and ring volume, not just media volume

Limits and versions

Use a low volume and take breaks to protect hearing. No acoustic test results are claimed.
