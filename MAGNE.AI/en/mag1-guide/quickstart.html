FIRST STEPS / Quick start

Prepare first.
Configure as needed.

93 new MAG1WW60-1 screenshots and 73 recordings cover 75 guide chapters. Browse current captures →

Already initialized devices can start at step 2. The initial setup wizard and packaging require separate on-device verification.

  1. Check the model, packaging and ports, inspect for damage and charge safely.
  2. Follow the language page to select English (United States) and match the English button labels.
  3. Prepare a trusted Wi-Fi network or suitable Nano-SIM. After connecting, verify internet access with an ordinary web page.
  4. Review Display, sound and notifications for your needs. Change one setting at a time and remember its original value.
  5. The owner should set up the screen lock and backups. Authentication and secret inputs are not recorded.
  6. Wallet and Web3 testing awaits an asset-free test environment. Check production and test app versions first.
