Network and connections / nfc

NFC, tapping cards and wallet recovery limits

Distinguish general NFC from wallet-card backup and recovery. NFC does not establish compatibility with every access, transit or payment card.

MAG1WW60-1 · dated captures available

This chapter uses MAG1WW60-1 evidence only. Reference steps are separate from the actions actually recorded.

Settings → Connected devices → Connection preferences → NFC

Before you start

  • Compatible NFC test card
  • Wallet testing requires an account with no real assets
  • This workflow has not been tested with a wallet

Reference steps · see recorded scope below

View dated screenshots and videos ↓

  1. Find NFC under Connection preferences. Confirm its controls against the captured device screen.
  2. Enable NFC using the actual page. Do not change the default payment app unless the owner explicitly chooses to do so.
  3. Use an app supporting the test card’s protocol. Follow its instructions to position the phone and card; the screen shows only the processing result.
  4. If the wallet offers NFC backup or recovery, first check its version, card compatibility and requirements. Those wallet controls have not been opened or verified.
  5. Without a test account, stop at instructions and preparation. Do not create, import, restore or overwrite a real wallet.

MAG1WW60-1 observed scope

Menu visibility and recorded navigation only; no setting changes.

Cancel, exit and restore

Turn off NFC if it was enabled solely for testing. Card writes and wallet recovery may not be easily reversible and are not performed here.

Troubleshooting

  • Card unresponsive: check protocol, app, switch, card orientation and phone position
  • Wallet does not recognize the card: first check card compatibility and app version
  • Card already contains data: do not overwrite a real backup card

Limits and versions

Wallet card backup and recovery have not been tested. Screen recording alone cannot establish that a physical card tap occurred or recovery succeeded.

16 October supplement · MAG1WW60-1 development software

  • HED NFC and SE services exist, but an external NFC backup card, the embedded SE and the platform TEE have distinct roles. No card model, backup/restore operation, lockout policy or cross-device recovery was validated in this report.
  • The wallet app handles decrypted recovery phrases during display/backup. Do not describe NFC backup as proof that recovery phrases never enter Android. Use only vendor-confirmed cards and wait for the analyzed build’s security fixes and recovery verification.
