MAG1 HYBRID AI ARCHITECTURE

Cloud AI assists.On-device AI keeps intelligence local.

Google Gemini works together with MAG1 on-device intelligence. Gemini serves as the default system AI assistant for overseas versions; Qwen2.5 1.5B, ASR and TTS are responsible for local translation and voice links, and real-time images are processed by UNISOC visual algorithms.

IVEXTENSIBLE AI

Development capabilities · SDK/AAR

End-side capabilities are handed over to applications.
Hardware details are left to the platform.

Business apps do not need to directly manage model files, NPU clock speeds or underlying drivers. After integrating FIH SDK / AAR, the application submits text or voice requests through a unified capability interface, and FIH AI Services takes over the model loading, service life cycle and inference routing, and then calls the local computing power of MAG1 through the client-side runtime.

01BUSINESS APP→ 02FIH SDK / AAR→ 03FIH AI SERVICES→ 04UNIAI / NN HAL→ 05CPU · GPU · NPU
LOCAL MODELLlmService

Manage local Qwen2.5 1.5B model calls; the current capability caliber focuses on end-side translation and is not packaged as an independent AI assistant.

TRANSLATIONTranslateService

Organize translation requests, language input and result returns, and connect local language models and voice links.

SPEECH TO TEXTAsrService

Receive audio and convert speech to text on the device, providing input for call translation and voice functions.

TEXT TO SPEECHTtsService

Convert text results into playable speech to complete the final path of terminal-side speech output.

APP CALLThe application calls the software capability interface provided by FIH and does not directly control the AX3596.

RUNTIME ROUTINGAt runtime, the workload is assigned to the CPU, GPU, or NPU based on the model, operator, and backend support status.

RESULTThe inference results are returned to the business app through the service layer, allowing the app to focus on product processes and user experience.

MAGNE.AI can provide SDK/AAR components to allow business apps to call MAG1's client-side model capabilities according to the integration scope. This is an extensible platform capability and does not mean it is currently fully open to all third-party applications.

ROADMAP / WEB3 NATIVE COMPUTE

Bring local computing power into Web3 Apps and DApps.

MAGNE.AI is promoting the integration of client-side AI and Web3 applications, allowing authorized Apps/DApps to invoke local translation, voice, intent understanding, and Agent workflows based on usage scenarios. Sensitive transactions still must go through user confirmation, device identity, and eSE hardware signature boundaries; AI is responsible for understanding and assisting and does not bypass authorization.
