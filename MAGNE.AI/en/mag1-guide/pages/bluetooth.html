Network and connections / bluetooth

Bluetooth pairing, disconnection and forgetting devices

Connect supported headphones, keyboards or other Bluetooth accessories after checking their identity.

MAG1WW60-1 · dated captures available

This chapter uses MAG1WW60-1 evidence only. Reference steps are separate from the actions actually recorded.

Settings → Connected devices → Pair new device

Before you start

  • A Bluetooth accessory available for testing
  • Accessory in discoverable or pairing mode
  • Do not publish personal paired-device names

Reference steps · see recorded scope below

  1. Open Connected devices → Pair new device.
  2. Confirm the test accessory’s name in the list. Do not select an unfamiliar device.
  3. If pairing codes appear on both devices, check that they match before confirming. Code entry depends on the accessory.
  4. For headphones, play non-sensitive test audio. For a keyboard, enter non-personal test text to check its intended function.
  5. Previously paired devices may be selected under Saved devices. The exact connection button still needs subpage review.
  6. For a temporary disconnection, use Disconnect or turn off the accessory. Forget removes the saved pairing and requires pairing again next time.

Recorded entry and controls

Connected devices, Pair new device and Connection preferences are shown. Bluetooth remained off. No discovery, pairing, saved-device inspection or audio connection was performed.

MAG1WW60-1 observed scope

Bluetooth entry and connection preferences only. Bluetooth remained off; no scan, pairing or audio connection was tested.

After inspection

Return using Back. This capture did not change any network or security setting.

Troubleshooting

  • Device not found: restart its pairing mode and check distance
  • Paired but silent: check media-audio routing and accessory volume
  • Codes differ: cancel and reject the connection
