TROUBLESHOOTING / Diagnose an issue

Find the cause.
Change one thing.

Record the time, version, error message and reproduction steps. Stop before authentication, payment, deletion or reset. Clearing data should not be the first step.

SymptomFirst checksRelated steps
No internet connectionCheck airplane mode and Internet first, then SIM, VPN, DNS and captive-portal sign-in. Change one item at a time.Open guide →
Delayed notificationsCheck app notifications → channels → Do Not Disturb → Battery startup/lock-screen restrictions → network.Open guide →
Screen hard to read or turns off quicklyCheck brightness, adaptive brightness, Screen timeout and accessibility display options under Display.Open guide →
Unexpected sound or vibrationDistinguish media, calls, ringtones, notifications and alarms; check headphone output and Do Not Disturb.Open guide →
USB only chargesCheck the data cable, device unlock, USB purpose and computer recognition. A charging-only cable cannot transfer data.Open guide →
Bluetooth device not foundPut the accessory in pairing mode and check distance and identity. Cancel immediately if pairing codes differ.Open guide →
Insufficient storageInspect Storage categories first, then handle only content with verified backups. Do not clear wallet data.Open guide →
Wallet will not open or recovery failsPreserve data and check the package and version. Stop before confirmation if a test account or authentication is unavailable.Open guide →
Different system versionCompare the actual build and region and recheck entries. This review does not install updates or flash firmware.Open guide →
Liquid ingress, damage or abnormal heatStop use and charging and contact official support. Current specifications do not promise immersion protection.Open guide →

Before contacting support

  • Product model and build; app name, package and version.
  • Error text, time, network/accessory conditions and the shortest reproduction steps.
  • Screenshots without personal data; share necessary order details only through private official channels.
  • Do not send private keys, recovery phrases, passwords or verification codes, or publish personal identification.

The official Contact page lists official@magne.ai for product support. No external message was sent. Warranty duration and service terms await a formal policy.
