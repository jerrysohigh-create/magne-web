Digital Wellbeing, app limits and parental controls

Use this chapter to prepare and check the digital wellbeing, app limits and parental controls workflow on this firmware.

Entry checked · Operation pending

MAG1WW53 · Android 15 · 2026-10-14

Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls

Before you start

  • Use your own profile; parental setup requires the appropriate guardian account.

Reference steps

  1. Inspect the dashboard and App timers, then choose a test app and a suitable daily limit.
  2. Review Bedtime mode and Focus mode schedules separately. App timers reset at midnight; remove a test timer after checking it.
  3. Inspect Screen time reminders, Heads Up and Show icon in the app list; change only the option needed.
  4. For a family device, read Parental controls setup before linking accounts; ordinary app timers are not proof of supervised-account restrictions.
