System settings / battery

Battery saving, background restrictions and auto-start

Understand standard battery saving and manufacturer background controls when investigating delayed notifications or stopped apps.

Entry verified · Operation awaiting verification

Baseline: MA1 · Android 15 · MAG1WW53. Captured: 2026-10-14. This page is an editorial draft. Steps have not been tested except where explicitly stated.

Settings → Battery

Before you start

  • Record the original settings
  • Check the impact before changing settings for phone, messaging, authenticator or security apps
  • Remaining battery life is an estimate

Steps

View this session's screenshots and videos ↓

  1. Open Battery and review charge level and Battery usage. Identify high-use sources before changing settings.
  2. Read Battery Saver and Ultra saving mode. Ultra saving may restrict available apps; it is not enabled on the user’s behalf in this session.
  3. Review Adaptive Battery; it is separate from restrictions applied to an individual app.
  4. Read the descriptions for App standby optimizer, Stop app after screen lock, Auto launch management and Power intensive apps.
  5. For delayed alerts, compare the app’s background permission, auto-start and stop-after-lock rules. Restore permission for a test app if needed.
  6. After a change, reproduce an alert with the same test app in foreground, background and locked states. Record battery level, network and elapsed time.

Expected result and recorded evidence

These manufacturer-specific menus were captured on the device. Background rules were not changed, and no battery-life improvement is claimed.

Cancel, exit and restore

Restore each app’s original rules and disable any saving mode enabled for the test. Do not demonstrate Ultra saving before its exit procedure is verified.

Troubleshooting

  • Delayed notifications: check network, notification permission, auto-start and stop-after-lock rules together
  • High battery use: first inspect usage duration and background activity
  • Battery-life prediction changes: the system recalculates it for the current workload

Limits and versions

Actual effects still need testing with an app containing no personal data. A battery-saving policy may affect alerts.
