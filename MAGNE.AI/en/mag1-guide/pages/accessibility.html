System settings / accessibility

Accessibility, text, colors and assisted controls

Choose features for your visual, hearing or interaction needs and learn how to exit before enabling unfamiliar gestures.

Entry verified · Operation awaiting verification

Baseline: MA1 · Android 15 · MAG1WW53. Captured: 2026-10-14. This page is an editorial draft. Steps have not been tested except where explicitly stated.

Settings → Accessibility

Before you start

  • Record the original settings
  • Read the exit instructions before enabling a screen reader, magnification or an accessibility menu
  • Use non-private content for testing

Steps

View this session's screenshots and videos ↓

  1. Open Accessibility and browse the categories and features available on the device.
  2. Start with easily reversible font or display size changes and inspect the sample text. Sliders and reset buttons require subpage verification.
  3. Choose color and animation options for your needs and record their original values before changing them.
  4. A screen reader changes tapping and swiping behavior. Do not enable it as a default demonstration before verifying how to exit.
  5. Hearing and interaction aids may affect audio channels, touch input and shortcuts. Read and test each item separately.
  6. Change one setting at a time and record the result. Restore it before trying another if it does not suit your needs.

Expected result and recorded evidence

Accessibility menus are inventoried on the device. The presence of an entry does not establish that its effects have passed testing.

Cancel, exit and restore

Restore the original options. Disable an accessibility service using its own instructions; do not assume a volume-key shortcut works for every service.

Troubleshooting

  • Enlarged page hides a button: use a verified accessibility exit method or go back
  • Tapping works differently: check whether a screen reader is active
  • Third-party service missing: check installation and permissions

Limits and versions

Only menu evidence is captured; compatibility with specialist assistive equipment is not established.
