Certificate Notes

Usage restrictions and functional notes recorded on the MAG1 EU-Type Examination Certificate.

The following notes originate from the original certificate and describe limitations and functional disclosures applicable to MAG1 when placed on the EU market.

1
When operating in the 5250–5350 MHz band, the device is restricted to indoor use only.
設備在 5250~5350 MHz 頻段操作時，僅限室內使用。
2
The transmitting antenna must be installed and operated with a minimum separation distance of at least 5 mm from all persons.
發射天線之安裝須與所有人員保持至少 5mm 之距離。
3
The device also supports radio frequency bands not designated for the EU.
設備亦支援非歐盟指定之射頻頻段。
4
The device also supports 5G NR n38 (HPUE), which was not assessed under the current version of the standard.
設備亦支援 5G NR n38 (HPUE)，該項於現行標準版本下未予評估。

Manufacturer Obligations (Summary)

Key obligations MAGNE's manufacturer must fulfil under RED 2014/53/EU and the EU-Type Examination regime.

The following is a concise summary of the manufacturer's principal obligations under the RED 2014/53/EU EU-Type Examination regime. The complete obligations are governed by EU regulation and the certificate itself.

§1
CE marking height must be at least 5 mm; the marking must also be visible on the packaging.
§2
Any change affecting compliance must be notified to NB 2907.
§3
The certificate and technical documentation must be retained by the manufacturer for 10 years.
