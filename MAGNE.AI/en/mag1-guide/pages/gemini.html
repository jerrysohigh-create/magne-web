MAGNE, AI and Web3 / Gemini

Gemini: side key, live screen sharing and webpage summaries

Open Gemini over a public webpage, share the screen in Live, or request a readable webpage summary.

Specified operations verified · Peer review pending

Verified on MAG1WW53: simulated power-key invocation, starting Live screen sharing, browsing the public MAG1 page, stopping sharing and ending Live; a separate Summarize page request produced an English response. Android media projection was checked both while active and after stopping. No spoken question or camera session was tested.

Press & hold power button → Gemini → Live / Summarize page

Gemini Live / Summarize page

Silent demonstration: microphone access is temporarily off, so the two Unblock microphone prompts are cancelled. Native touch markers, swipe trails and the coordinate strip are retained. The side-key press is simulated through ADB. No image masks or fabricated replies are used.

Before you start

  • An enabled Gemini app, Google as the digital assistant, an eligible signed-in account and a working network. Availability can differ by account, region and app version.
  • Use a public page. Close messages, account pages and sensitive notifications before sharing. The demonstration uses the MAG1 English product page.
  • Check Settings → System → Gestures → Press & hold power button → Digital assistant. This device already had Digital assistant selected; no change was needed.

Steps

  1. Open the MAG1 English product page in Chrome. Press and hold the side power button. Gemini appears over the page. The recording uses an ADB-simulated long press; it does not film a physical finger or test the physical button.
  2. For live sharing, select the Live icon at the lower right. This device also offers Share screen with Live. In the recorded route, the Live controls are opened first, then Screenshare.
  3. Read the Android sharing dialog. This recording uses Entire screen → Start. Anything visible while sharing may be sent to Google; do not switch to private content.
  4. Return to Chrome, scroll the public page, then tap the floating Gemini bubble to return to the Live controls. Tap Screenshare again to stop sharing, then the X button to end Live.
  5. For a text result, invoke Gemini over the public page again and select Summarize page. Wait for the English response, then scroll to read it. This sends the webpage context for a summary; it is a separate operation from continuous Live sharing.
  6. Compare the generated answer with the source page. The captured answer is an AI-generated summary of website claims, not independent validation of product specifications or capabilities.

Expected result and recorded evidence

Verified on MAG1WW53: simulated power-key invocation, starting Live screen sharing, browsing the public MAG1 page, stopping sharing and ending Live; a separate Summarize page request produced an English response. Android media projection was checked both while active and after stopping. No spoken question or camera session was tested.

Cancel, exit and restore

Stop Screenshare, end Live and dismiss the assistant. For this authorized test, Gemini was temporarily enabled and then restored to its original disabled state. Temporary touch-display settings, microphone access and the original captions setting were restored. Assistant defaults were checked against the original values. Existing conversation history and account data were not opened or deleted.

Troubleshooting

  • Power menu appears: check the long-press setting and the default digital assistant.
  • Gemini is disabled: open its App info and enable it only if you want to use it. This test device was restored to disabled after capture.
  • No Live, screen-sharing or summary entry: check app/account/region availability and current Google documentation; the same firmware alone does not guarantee identical cloud features.
  • Microphone warning in this recording: system microphone access was intentionally blocked, and Cancel kept it blocked. The Live microphone was also muted. These privacy prompts are part of the silent demo, not a Gemini failure.
  • The reply differs: model output and webpage content can change. Check the source rather than treating the recording as a fixed answer.

Limits and versions

Captured 2026-10-14 on MAG1WW53 / Android 15, with Gemini 1.0.795460806 and Google app 17.64.22.ve.arm64. The installed Gemini package has the Android SYSTEM flag on this unit; this does not establish the app state of every retail device. Recheck both firmware and Google/Gemini versions after updates. Cloud inference, physical-button reliability, voice quality, camera use, offline operation and connected personal services are outside this verification.

This is a feature demonstration, not a claim of Google endorsement or a test of every Gemini capability.
