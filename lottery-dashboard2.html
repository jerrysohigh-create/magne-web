🛡️ Invitation & Anti-Abuse Rules

  1. Referral rewards apply only to first-level direct invitations. No second-, third-, or multi-level rewards exist.
  2. After the invitee completes their first valid participation, the invitation relationship cannot be changed.
  3. The official default entry point does not generate referral rewards and is not counted toward the leaderboard.
  4. Secondary market MS2 purchases, secondary market trading volume, or external MS2 transfers do not generate direct referral rewards.
  5. Self-invitation, bots, wash trading, circular funds, same-controller bulk addresses, sybil behavior, or other abnormal patterns may result in reward cancellation and disqualification.
  6. Large rewards, leaderboard eligibility, and reward pool distribution may require additional eligibility review, KYC/AML compliance, and applicable regional restrictions.

All rules above are subject to official campaign rules, eligibility review, anti-abuse checks, TGE timing, regional restrictions, and applicable regulations.
