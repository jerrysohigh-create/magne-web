I

CERTIFICATE ROLE

What problem does this information solve?

platformAndroid 15 + GMS
Content protectionWidevine L1
useGoogle Ecosystem Access and Cooperation Review
data hierarchyPublic PDF + Partner Reference

The GMS reference is not a single certificate, but a set of supporting records related to Android compatibility, Google Play ecosystem, authorization and device information.

Widevine L1 is used to indicate that the device has high-level content protection capabilities and is usually reviewed by streaming video, media compatibility and platform partners.

G0 Explanatory Note serves as an entry explanatory document to lower the threshold for reviewers to read the entire package of Google-related PDFs for the first time.

II

EVIDENCE ROUTE

How to check.

01Read the G0 Explanatory Note first

Understand the boundaries, purpose and reading order of this set of documents.

02Check Android / Partner / Regional information

Basic information required to confirm platform compatibility and collaborative review.

03Check out Widevine L1 related information again

Interpret content protection capabilities together with device platform information.
