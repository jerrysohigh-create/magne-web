Calls and messages / phone

Phone, dialing, answering and in-call controls

Prepare and verify phone functions step by step without reading real contacts or call history.

Installation and version verified · Interface awaiting verification

Baseline: MA1 · Android 15 · MAG1WW53. Captured: 2026-10-14. This page is an editorial draft. Steps have not been tested except where explicitly stated.

App list → Phone; Settings → Network & internet → SIMs

Before you start

  • Test contacts without real personal numbers and an owner-approved test number
  • Carrier service and authorization for call charges
  • Do not call emergency numbers for testing

Steps

  1. The Google Phone package and version are confirmed. Its home screen containing real call history is not opened for capture.
  2. In a test environment, check the dial pad, default SIM selection, call button, answering and rejecting.
  3. Once a test call connects, separately test speaker, mute, keypad, hold and other buttons actually shown. Recording and conference support depend on the region, app and carrier and are not assumed.
  4. After ending the call, inspect the test call record and establish deletion options and effects. Deleting records requires separate confirmation.
  5. Check whether settings actually provide number blocking, caller ID and spam protection.

Buttons and options

Button / optionPurpose and limits
MutePrevents the other party from hearing the microphone; separate from speaker volume
SpeakerChanges the audio output
End callEnds the current call
Recording / conferenceAvailability requires regional and service verification

Expected result and recorded evidence

App installation and version are verified. No outgoing call, incoming call or call recording was tested.

Cancel, exit and restore

Use End call to finish a test. Do not delete real records or change the block list.

Troubleshooting

  • Cannot call: check the SIM, service, airplane mode and plan
  • Cannot hear: check the output route, volume and network
  • Wrong SIM used: check the default and selection before dialing

Limits and versions

A test number, counterpart and approval for charges are required. A menu option does not establish carrier support.
