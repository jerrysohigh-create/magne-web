FCC Authorization Scope

Summary of tested categories and their coverage within the FCC authorization for MAG1.

Category Coverage
Licensed Cellular GSM, WCDMA, LTE, and 5G NR across Part 22(H), 24(E), 27(L) and related sub-parts. Multiple RF reports cover conducted output power, ERP/EIRP, band edge, spurious emissions, and frequency stability.
2.4GHz Wireless Bluetooth BR/EDR/LE and 2.4GHz WLAN included in the FCC device scope and associated RF and SAR testing files.
5GHz WLAN / U-NII 5GHz WLAN operation under Part 15 Subpart E §15.407, with separate U-NII and DFS reports. DFS tested as client device without radar detection — channel move time, channel closing transmission time, and non-occupancy period all PASS.
NFC NFC at 13.56 MHz explicitly included in the equipment scope and device-class grant path.
EMC / Digital Device Part 15 Subpart B AC conducted emissions and radiated emissions — both reported as PASS with under-limit margins.
RF Exposure / SAR Completed under 47 CFR Part 2 (2.1093) and applicable KDB procedures. Covers head SAR, hotspot SAR, body-worn accessory, product-specific 10g SAR, and simultaneous transmission scenarios.

On Equipment Scope in the Part 15B Report

The Part 15B EMC report for MAG1 explicitly lists the device's supported radio and application features: GSM/WCDMA/LTE/5G NR, WLAN 2.4GHz and 5GHz, Bluetooth, GNSS, NFC, and WPT — confirming that the FCC authorization scope extends across the full wireless capability set of the device.
