System settings / display-enhancement

Color, video enhancement, SDR/HDR and backlight power saving

Distinguish display processing from camera recording. Display enhancement is not camera HDR or native HDR video.

Entry verified · Operation awaiting verification

Baseline: MA1 · Android 15 · MAG1WW53. Captured: 2026-10-14. This page is an editorial draft. Steps have not been tested except where explicitly stated.

Settings → Display → Colors & contrast / Video display enhancement / SDR to HDR video enhancement / Backlight power saving

Before you start

  • The same non-private test video or image
  • Record the original switches
  • Do not promise visual improvements or color accuracy

Steps

  1. Scroll down in Display to Colors & contrast and read the available modes. This review opens the menu without selecting a new mode.
  2. Video display enhancement and SDR to HDR video enhancement are display-processing entries. They do not establish that a file has become HDR.
  3. For an inline switch, tapping the label may immediately toggle it. Capture automation skips recognized switches rather than changing them for a screenshot.
  4. Compare before and after using the same file, brightness and app, recording the mode, temperature and compatibility conditions.
  5. Read the on-device explanation and test Backlight power saving. Its name alone does not establish longer battery life.
  6. Restore all original modes after testing.

Expected result and recorded evidence

The entries were seen and Colors & contrast was opened. Enhancements were not enabled; image quality and power use were not measured.

Cancel, exit and restore

Restore the original color mode and enhancement/backlight switches.

Troubleshooting

  • Unexpected colors: restore the original modes one at a time
  • No change in a video: check the playback app and compatibility
  • Switch changed accidentally: record and restore that item without resetting the device

Limits and versions

Display enhancement and HDR recording are different features. This review draws no performance or image-quality conclusions.
