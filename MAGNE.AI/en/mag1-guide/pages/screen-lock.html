Security and recovery / screen-lock

Screen lock, fingerprints and authentication

Protect device access. Keep passwords, fingerprint enrollment and recovery credentials out of tutorial media.

Awaiting verification

Baseline: MA1 · Android 15 · MAG1WW53. Captured: 2026-10-14. This page is an editorial draft. Steps have not been tested except where explicitly stated.

Settings → Security & privacy → Device unlock (exact subpage awaiting verification)

Before you start

  • Owner present and able to use the current screen lock
  • Security credentials are not changed in this task
  • Fingerprint functionality requires verification on its actual subpage

Steps

  1. Find the device-unlock entry under Security & privacy and read the current status.
  2. For an authenticated subpage, the owner must enter the current credential. This capture series stops before authentication.
  3. Adding fingerprints, changing a PIN or password, or removing a lock requires separate explicit authorization and documented recovery conditions.
  4. After setup, the owner should test their fingerprint and fallback credential. Do not capture credential entry.
  5. A fallback credential may still be required after a restart or in other situations. Fingerprints are not the only recovery method to rely on.

Buttons and options

Button / optionPurpose and limits
Device unlockThe exact entry name requires deeper verification
Fallback credentialKept by the owner
FingerprintA hardware specification does not mean enrollment or recognition has been tested in this session

Expected result and recorded evidence

No authenticated page was entered, and no lock credential was changed.

Cancel, exit and restore

Cancel authentication and go back. Unlocking without data loss cannot be promised if credentials are forgotten.

Troubleshooting

  • Fingerprint not recognized: check sensor and finger cleanliness, then use the owner’s fallback credential
  • Lock forgotten: use official account or support procedures; do not bypass protection

Limits and versions

Authentication and credential changes are outside the tested scope. These are draft instructions without operational verification.
