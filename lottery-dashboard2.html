🛡️ Referral & Anti-Abuse Rules Referral Rules

  1. Referral rewards apply only to direct first-level referrals; there are no second-, third-, or multi-level rewards.
  2. The referral relationship cannot be changed after the invitee completes their first valid entry.
  3. The official default entry point generates no referral rewards and does not count toward the leaderboard.
  4. Market purchases, secondary-market volume, or externally transferred MS2 do not generate direct referral rewards.
  5. Self-referrals, bots, wash activity, circular funds, batches of commonly controlled addresses, Sybil behavior, or other anomalies may result in reward cancellation and loss of eligibility.
  6. Large rewards, leaderboard eligibility, and pool distributions may require additional eligibility review, KYC/AML requirements, and applicable regional restrictions.

All rules are subject to official campaign rules, eligibility and anti-abuse checks, the TGE schedule, regional restrictions, and applicable regulations. MAGNE.AI reserves final interpretation of the campaign rules.

What Is MS2?

MS2 ≠ MHA

MS2 is a Season 2 campaign credit/token used for campaign tracking, rankings, staking weight, and future eligibility reviews. MS2 is not MHA, and there is no fixed conversion ratio between them. MS2 does not represent a financial product, fixed return, guaranteed return, or listing right.

Staking Weight

Staking Weight
Effective Weight = MS2 Amount × Staking Duration Factor × Community Tier Factor

Staking weight is used only for campaign rankings, weight calculations, and future eligibility review; it does not represent fixed returns or guaranteed distributions.
