Camera and tools / camera

Camera, photos, videos and modes

Inventory modes and parameters from the current camera UI. Hardware HAL data or marketing claims do not substitute for available controls.

Installation and version verified · Interface awaiting verification

Baseline: MA1 · Android 15 · MAG1WW53. Captured: 2026-10-14. This page is an editorial draft. Steps have not been tested except where explicitly stated.

App list → Camera (com.android.camera2)

Before you start

  • A non-private test scene and sufficient storage
  • Installed camera version: 1.0000.18
  • Do not capture real gallery thumbnails, people or private indoor scenes

Steps

  1. Prepare a non-private test scene before opening the camera. This task does not photograph unknown surroundings.
  2. Inventory the actual controls for photo, video, front/rear switching, zoom, flash, timer, grid, aspect ratio, resolution and storage location.
  3. In video mode, verify available combinations of resolution, frame rate, HDR and stabilization, then record a test clip and inspect its file properties.
  4. Record front and rear camera options separately. Rear-camera specifications do not describe selfie features.
  5. After taking a test photo, check sharpness, orientation and storage path using the non-private test file.
  6. Do not add slow-motion or OIS instructions when absent from the menu. The official site currently does not list slow motion and states that OIS is unsupported.

The package version is verified. This review provides no new photo, video-mode or image-quality test evidence.

Cancel, exit and restore

Stop capture and go back. Before deleting output, confirm that it consists only of test files.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera will not open: check permissions and other apps using it
  • Capture stops: check storage, temperature and version
  • Setting missing: record the actual mode; do not automatically classify it as a hardware fault

Limits and versions

The official site lists rear video up to 4K30, front video up to FHD30, HDR and EIS. These are manufacturer descriptions, not results retested in this review.

Additional coverage · 14 October 2026

Historical test, 13 October 2026: selecting 4K 30 fps disabled HDR and EIS; turning HDR on changed the setting to 1080p 30 fps with EIS still off.
