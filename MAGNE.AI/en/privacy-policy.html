This privacy policy ("Privacy Policy") describes how MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited ("MAGNE.AI", "we" or "the Company") collects, uses, processes and discloses information related to you.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our handling and use of your personal information.

The data protection and privacy laws in the United States may differ from the laws in your country or region and may, in some cases, be less protective. By accessing or using the Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and acknowledge that such information will be subject to United States law, including applicable federal and state privacy regulations.

We will take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information in accordance with the requirements of U.S. law.

Information you provide directly to us

We collect personal information that you provide directly to MAGNE AI Global Tech Limited. This includes information you provide when you contact us, fill out a form, make a purchase, communicate with us through a third-party platform, participate in a contest or promotion, respond to a survey, submit feedback, request support or otherwise interact with us.

Categories of personal information we may collect include:
