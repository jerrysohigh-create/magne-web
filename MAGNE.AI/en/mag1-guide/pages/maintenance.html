Troubleshooting and maintenance / maintenance

Cleaning, stopping unsafe use and official support

Provide practical care instructions and sourced support contacts without inventing warranty periods or return conditions.

MAG1WW60-1 · demonstration pending

This chapter uses MAG1WW60-1 evidence only. Reference steps are separate from the actions actually recorded.

Device exterior → Official Contact page

Before you start

  • Stop unnecessary operations and disconnect cables
  • Keep cleaning liquid out of ports
  • After-sales terms depend on the purchase region, batch and formal policy

Reference steps · see recorded scope below

  1. Gently wipe the casing with a soft, dry cloth. Do not pour liquid into ports, disassemble the device or repair the battery yourself.
  2. Stop use and charging if there is liquid ingress, swelling, unusual odor, smoke or visible damage. Keep the device away from heat and contact professional service.
  3. The current official specification is IP52. Do not rely on the old alpha PDF's IP67 claim for immersion use.
  4. Gather the model, system/app versions, incident time, reproduction steps and an error screenshot without private information.
  5. Confirm official@magne.ai through the official Contact page. When contacting support, include the model, software version and a description of the issue. Do not include passwords or recovery phrases.
  6. Request the repair, warranty, shipping and return terms for the relevant region. Do not promise a “one-year global warranty” without a formal policy.
