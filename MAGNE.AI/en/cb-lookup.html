What problem does this information solve?

issue certificateTUV Rheinland Japan Ltd.
useBattery Safety and Market Access Reference

CB Scheme is an international mutual recognition mechanism for electrical product qualification testing and certification, which can support safety review in multiple countries.

MAG1-related CB documents focus on battery safety, including test backgrounds such as short circuit, abnormal charging, thermal abuse, and mechanical shock.

CB documents should be understood separately from UN38.3 transport safety and market access information such as CE/FCC, but together form a complete device compliance view.
